Smart Streetlight System

        
The Smart Street Light is an IoT-based system that automatically adjusts street-light brightness according to traffic and ambient light conditions. It uses an LDR to detect day/night and a motion/vehicle sensor to detect passing vehicles. The lights remain dim when the road is empty and switch to full brightness when a vehicle is detected, helping save energy and improve road safety.
